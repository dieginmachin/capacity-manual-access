Capacity Calculator

One honest number before you change anything: unique designs per business day for your team, and what each email costs you. It's a Claude skill. Install it once, then hand it your task export.

Download the skill (.zip) →Read chapter 01

Bookmark this page. It's your private link, and it's also in your email. Please don't share it.

  1. 01

    Install it in Claude

    Claude app (claude.ai or desktop): Settings → Capabilities. Turn on code execution, then under Skills choose Upload skill and pick capacity-calculator.zip.

    Claude Code: unzip it into ~/.claude/skills/capacity-calculator/.

    2 minNeeds Claude with code execution
  2. 02

    Try it on the example

    In a new chat: "Run the capacity calculator on the example tasks with a monthly cost of 9000." The example is made-up data, so you can see the report before using your own.

    1 min
  3. 03

    Export last month's tasks

    ClickUp: open the List or Space with your design tasks → ⋯ → Export → CSV, all columns. If ClickUp is connected to Claude, skip the export and say "pull last month's finished design tasks from ClickUp".

    Any other tool works if the CSV has a task name and a done date.

    3 min
  4. 04

    Run your baseline

    Attach the export and say: "Run my capacity baseline for last month. Our monthly design cost is …" You get unique designs, revisions, designs per business day by month, cost per email, and a saved baseline file.

    2 min
  5. 05

    Write it down. Run it again next month.

    Keep capacity-baseline.json. Next month, say "compare with my last baseline". The skill reports the change, and asks what else changed in between. Correlation, not proof of cause.

The rules it follows, from chapter 01
  • Unique designs, not rows. A repeated task name is a revision.
  • Business days only, minus your holidays.
  • Team numbers. Per person only when your export supports it.
  • Results, not goals. No industry averages. Your own baseline is the only comparison.
